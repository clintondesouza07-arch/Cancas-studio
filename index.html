Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 16 November 2026 · CANCAS STUDIO

This Privacy Policy explains how CANCAS STUDIO ("we", "us"), a web design & development studio based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit cancasstudio.com, contact us, or become a client.

1. What We Collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone / WhatsApp number, business type, package and hosting choice, and the project details you write.
  • Chat assistant (Amaya): the messages you type. If you use the "free quote" helper, your answers, name, WhatsApp number and email are saved as an enquiry.
  • Analytics (only if you accept cookies): pages visited, time on site, device and browser type, and approximate location, collected by Google Analytics.
  • Abuse protection: to stop spam, our chat keeps a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for up to 24 hours. We never store the IP address itself.
  • Clients: the contact, project, billing, payment and signed-agreement details needed to deliver and invoice our services.

2. How We Use It

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and talk to you about your project.
  • To build, host and support your website, and to issue invoices and agreements.
  • To understand how visitors use our website and improve it (with your consent).
  • To keep our website, forms and chat secure and free from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who We Share It With

We only share data with trusted service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Supabase: secure database for enquiries, client records and agreements.
  • Web3Forms: delivers contact-form messages to our inbox.
  • Google: Google Analytics (only if you accept cookies), and Google's Gemini AI, which writes the chat assistant's replies. Messages you type in the chat are sent to Gemini to generate a reply.
  • WhatsApp / email providers: when you choose to contact us there.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UAE. We choose providers that protect personal data with appropriate security measures. We may also disclose data where required by law.

4. Cookies & Similar Technology

We use Google Analytics cookies (such as _ga) only after you tap "Accept" on our cookie banner. If you decline, analytics does not load. We also save your cookie choice and a few small settings in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. We do not use advertising cookies. You can change your choice any time via Cookie Settings.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep personal data only as long as we need it to respond to you, deliver our services, and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Enquiries from people who do not go on to become clients are reviewed and deleted periodically. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate
  • Delete your data
  • Stop or restrict how we use it, or withdraw your consent (including for cookies)

Email hello@cancasstudio.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, access-controlled databases and admin-only logins to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to those who need it.

8. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this website, with the effective date shown above.
