About Us

WHO IS
CANCAS STUDIO

A web design and development studio in Dubai, building custom websites for businesses that want to be found, trusted, and contacted.

Cancas Studio is a web design and web development studio based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We design, build, host and maintain custom websites for businesses across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider UAE.

We operate as an online studio. Every project runs remotely — from the first discovery call through design, development and launch — so there is no office visit required and no travel time built into your quote. Enquiries come in by email, phone or WhatsApp, and work is delivered the same way.

Once a site is built, we keep running it. Our managed plans include hosting, a domain and ongoing updates, so clients are never handed a login and left to work out servers, renewals or plugin updates for themselves.
